THE RULES

One word. One imposter.

  1. Everyone gets the same secret word, except the undercover player.
  2. Take turns giving a one-word clue. Say enough to prove you know it, but not so much that you give it away.
  3. Discuss, then vote for the player you think is undercover. A tied vote gets one revote.
  4. The group wins by finding the undercover. The undercover wins when only one other player remains.

Best with 4–10 people. Keep your screen to yourself.
